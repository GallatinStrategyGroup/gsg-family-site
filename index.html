What families
ask us first.

What is an independent educational consultant?
An IEC guides students through the college search and application process. They work with a small number of families — not the hundreds a school counselor manages — and aren't affiliated with any college.
How much does a college counselor cost?
Fees vary by experience, scope, and your student's specific needs. Independent counselors typically work on retainer, with packages ranging from a few thousand dollars to more comprehensive multi-year engagements.
How much does the matching call cost?
The matching call is $200. That's a one-time fee for the 30–60 minute conversation where we learn about your student and make our recommendation. There are no additional charges from GSG after that.
When should we start?
Earlier is almost always better — many families connect with a counselor in 9th or 10th grade. That said, we work with families at all stages, including those starting in 11th or 12th grade.
What happens on the matching call?
A 30–60 minute conversation, not a sales call. We ask about your student's goals and timeline, then identify the right counselor and make a direct introduction.
What if the match isn't right?
Tell us. We'll revisit at no extra charge. A good match is about working style as much as credentials — we're not done until it feels right.
How is this different from a directory?
A directory lists everyone. We make one specific recommendation based on your student's situation — one considered introduction, not a list to research on your own.
